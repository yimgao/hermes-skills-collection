## Notes & caveats

1. **Many states have eliminated emissions testing** since 2020 due to improved vehicle emissions. Verify your county/city.
2. **Inspection vs emissions** — they're often separate programs with separate stations and fees.
3. **New cars are usually exempt** for the first 2–8 years depending on state.
5. **DMV fee structures** differ wildly ($5 in some states, $200+ in others, plus county add-ons).
6. **Online renewal** is available in most states — never pay a third-party "DMV services" upcharge.
7. **Grace periods** range from 0 to 60 days; check before relying on a buffer.
8. **Out-of-state moves** typically require re-registration within 30 days.

When the skill records a calendar item, it prompts the user for their state's actual rules. If the user says *"unsure"*, the skill defaults to **annual** with a reminder 60 days out.
